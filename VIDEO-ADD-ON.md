# Video & Motion — the add-on

> **Not installed by default. On purpose.**

Running a Training Club means members, programming, retention and race prep. Video is a
different job, and bundling a whole video pipeline into every install made setup longer
for every operator in order to serve the few who publish regularly.

So [AI_NAME] asks once, and only installs it if you say yes.

---

## The button

Say any of these to [AI_NAME] — now, or in six months:

> *"I need the video add-on, go ahead."*
> *"Add video."* · *"Set up the video tools."*

[AI_NAME] fetches and runs the add-on's own install playbook:

```
Install the Video & Motion add-on from
https://github.com/DanJoachimn/Partner-Ai-Kit-Video —
read INSTALL.md and walk me through it.
```

~15 minutes. It asks before each piece; skipping is a normal answer.

---

## Should a Training Club say yes?

**Probably yes if you:**
- post exercise demos or technique breakdowns to Instagram or TikTok
- film race recaps after a HYROX weekend
- record onboarding or programming explainers for members
- want long coaching videos or podcasts transcribed instead of re-watched

**Probably no if you:**
- shoot on a phone and post straight from it, happily
- have a coach or an agency already handling content

You can add it later at no cost. Nothing else in your kit depends on it.

---

## What's in it

- **Watch** — any video becomes a transcript and a summary
- **Edit** — cut, trim and assemble real footage; find music, sound effects, voiceover
- **Animate** — motion graphics rendered from code, no timeline dragging
- **Generate** — images and clips via 1200+ AI models
- **The pre-production rules** — decisions to lock *before* you record, because most of
  them can't be fixed afterwards. Worth ten minutes even if you install nothing else.

**Cost:** the skills are free. A couple of optional paid tools (image generation, premium
voices) bill per use — free tiers shown first.
